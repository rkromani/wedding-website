FAQ

What should I wear?

We want people to wear what they will feel great in! There’s no minimum or maximum level of fancyness we’re looking for, as long as you feel festive. Whether it’s a mini-dress and gogo boots, your favorite print shirt and a bolo tie, or a ball gown and tennis shoes, as long as you can dance the night away we’ll be happy.

What’s the weather going to be like?

We expect daytime temps in the high 70s-80s, and evenings cooling into the low 60s, so please feel free to dress appropriately for the weather, and bring layers for the evenings! We’ll update this with exact forecasts closer to the date.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation will list everyone we've reserved a seat for. If you have questions, just reach out to us, we may have missed something.

Are kids welcome?

Kiddos are very welcome, and we’ll have a child-friendly area set up as well. Please reach out to us if you're looking to bring kids!

What about dogs?

We love dogs, but ask that guests leave their dogs, cats, turtles, and any amphibious pets at home. Llamas, porcupines, and small humpback whale breeds are welcome with an official Emotional Support Animal (ESA) letter.

How do we get around town?

Given that Winthrop is a small community with limited public transportation, we reccomend that guests drive or rent a car if they are flying in. We will have shuttles to and from the town to the wedding venue on the day of the wedding for those who do not want to drive.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes! Access to the venue is via a privately maintained gravel road that should be passable to virtually all vehicles in good conditions. For those not camping, be prepared for a short walk up to the cabin from the parking area. You’re welcome to drop people off at the top, but turnaround space is limited—and the walk is worth it! All vehicles should be parked near the pump house - at the bottom of the driveway (signage will be posted), and someone will be there to help guide you. We’ll be arranging shuttles at various points throughout the weekend, so be on the lookout for more information and a survey.

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP by February 1, 2026. In the meantime, please feel free to submit a tentative RSVP and Lodging form, this will help us plan lodging, catering, etc., you can always edit it later!
